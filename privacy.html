Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

🔒 Short version: Your PDF files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser. We don't see, store, or have access to any files you use on PDFSnip.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

PDFSnip is built on a privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, store, or process:

  • Any PDF files you work with
  • The contents of your documents
  • File names or metadata from your documents
  • Personal identification information

2. How It Works

All PDF processing (splitting, merging, compressing) is performed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. Your files are loaded into browser memory, processed locally, and the result is offered as a download — all without any network transfer of file data.

3. Advertising

PDFSnip displays advertisements to keep the service free. We use Monetag as our advertising partner. Monetag may collect anonymous usage data such as IP address, browser type, and ad interaction data for the purposes of serving relevant advertisements. Please refer to Monetag's Privacy Policy for details on their data practices.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use minimal cookies strictly necessary for site functionality. Third-party advertising partners may set their own cookies for ad delivery and frequency capping. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

PDFSnip uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (no personal data shared)
  • Monetag — for advertising (subject to their privacy policy)

6. Children's Privacy

PDFSnip is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: hello@pdfsnip.net
